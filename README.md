The one line a smart fridge texts its owner at 3am: a small machine, awake all night, finally filing a complaint about the nightly ritual of opening the fridge just to stare into it.

The line:

> 3am. Fourth night in a row you've opened me just to stare. I'm a fridge, not a therapist.

Short enough to read on a lock screen, complete without a follow-up, and fresh: no milk, no ice, no door left open — just a fridge with an opinion at 3am.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes a single 89-character line to `/output/entry.txt`.
